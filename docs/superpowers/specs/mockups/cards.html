Bertahan Setelah Layoff

Rules for all cards

  • Depth from stacked edges, layered shadow, tape and tilt. A real 2px edge around every card (3:1 on the page).
  • One link per card (the title); the whole card may be clickable with a stretched link, never nested links.
  • Tilt at most 1.5 degrees. Hover and focus lift and straighten; both are off under prefers-reduced-motion. Text is never on a texture or ruled line for long reading.
  • Heading level is a prop so page heading order stays valid. Category and kind are text labels.
