# Depth

The depth layer (`src/styles/components/depth.css`). Everything here is decoration, text colors come from checked tokens.

## Keys of the header size

Use the shared `NavKey` for every navigation key, choice and toggle: exactly 48px tall, icon keys exactly 48 x 48 (`icon`), round optional (`round`), `tight` for narrow grids. A selected key is pressed in. Keys that mean wayfinding or on/off state also light a small lamp on the top rim (`lamp`); filters and pagination change color instead. Never an underline.

## Sheet (long reading goes here)

`.sheet`: solid paper on top of the textured page, with a real edge and thickness. Body text, headings and links are checked on it at AAA (7:1) in every palette.

<div class="sheet not-prose"><p class="m-0">Text on a sheet. The page behind has a texture, this does not.</p></div>

## Titles

`.b-h1`, `.title-3d` and prose `h1` are extruded in the palette color (the offset scales with the font size). `.b-h2`, `.b-h3`, `.shelf-title` are letterpress.
